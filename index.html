My working principles

Good results need more than a high score. I pay attention to data leakage, reproducible splits, clear baselines, and documenting what the evaluation actually shows.

01
Start with a baseline

Build a simple, interpretable starting point before adding complexity.

02
Validate rigorously

Use appropriate metrics and protect the validation process from leakage.

03
Make it usable

Where practical, turn a model into a demo people can interact with.
